# Gymtrack

A complete platform for training plan management and physical evolution tracking, connecting athletes and personal trainers in one place.

This repository contains the Gymtrack API code, responsible for exposing the services and business rules consumed by client applications (mobile, web, etc.).

## Overview

Gymtrack unifies body metrics analysis, workout execution, and trainer-student connection, making life easier for both those who train and those who prescribe workouts.

## Key Features

### Training Plans Management

- Creation of detailed training plans (Hypertrophy, Strength, Mixed) at different difficulty levels: beginner, intermediate, and advanced.
- Structuring of training days with exercises, sets, reps, demonstration videos, and notes.
- Workout log: daily progress tracking with load and rep annotations.
- Plan subscription system, ratings with reviews, and invites for private participants.

### Evolution & Biometrics

- Distinct profiles for Athletes and Personal Trainers, with direct linking between them.
- Body weight and measurement tracking (chest, waist, arms, thighs, body fat percentage, muscle mass, etc.).
- Dashboards with evolution charts, training adherence rate, weekly frequency, active plan progress, and health insights.
- Granular privacy controls over which data is shared with the trainer.

### Community

- Follower system to build and engage with the fitness community.

## Target Audience

- **Athletes / Practitioners:** who want to monitor their progress, log loads, and execute workouts with guidance.
- **Personal Trainers:** who need a tool to prescribe customized training plans, closely track their students, and analyze evolution metrics.
